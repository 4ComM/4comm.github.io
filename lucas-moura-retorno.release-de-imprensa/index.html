Lucas Moura voltou em 160 dias, e o São Paulo venceu os dois jogos com ele em campoLucas Moura was back in 160 days, and São Paulo won both games with him on the pitch

Lucas Moura · Meia-atacante · 34 anos · São PauloLucas Moura · Attacking midfielder · 34 · São Paulo

Lucas Moura voltou a jogar em 10 de outubro, 160 dias depois de romper o tendão de Aquiles contra o Bahia (3 de maio). Entrou aos 59 minutos da vitória por 2 a 1 sobre o Vitória e, no sábado seguinte (17), jogou o segundo tempo da vitória por 1 a 0 sobre o Vasco: 76 minutos em dois jogos (31 e 45), com duas vitórias. Com ele em campo, o São Paulo fez 2 gols e não sofreu nenhum.Lucas Moura played again on October 10, 160 days after rupturing his Achilles tendon against Bahia (May 3). He came on in the 59th minute of the 2-1 win over Vitória and, the following Saturday (17th), played the second half of the 1-0 win over Vasco: 76 minutes across two games (31 and 45), with two wins. With him on the pitch, São Paulo scored 2 goals and conceded none.

As previsões publicadas em maio variaram: de 4 a 6 meses (DGABC, 04/05) a 7 a 12 meses (CNN Brasil, 03/05) e 8 a 12 meses (EFE, 04/05), e o OneFootball escreveu que ele voltaria “apenas em 2027”. O cirurgião, Moisés Cohen, via como ideal, biologicamente, treinar em campo só depois de seis meses. Lucas treinou com o elenco 144 dias após a cirurgia.Forecasts published in May varied: from 4 to 6 months (DGABC, May 4) to 7 to 12 months (CNN Brasil, May 3) and 8 to 12 months (EFE, May 4), and OneFootball wrote that he would be back “only in 2027”. The surgeon, Moisés Cohen, saw training on the pitch only after six months as the biological ideal. Lucas trained with the squad 144 days after surgery.

No Brasileirão, nos 10 jogos em que entrou, o São Paulo somou 23 dos 30 pontos (76,7%), contra 30,2% nos 21 jogos sem ele. São poucos jogos, e em 5 deles Lucas jogou menos de 30 minutos (a leitura completa está no card “Em campo”). Quando ele se lesionou, o São Paulo era o 4º colocado; com os jogos até 17/10, é o 8º.In the Brasileirão, in the 10 games in which he played, São Paulo took 23 of 30 points (76.7%), against 30.2% in the 21 games without him. These are few games, and in 5 of them Lucas played under 30 minutes (the full reading is in the “On the pitch” card). When he was injured São Paulo were 4th; with games up to October 17, they are 8th.

Segundo o clube, cada fase só começou depois que a anterior foi cumprida, conforme a resposta do tendão às cargas de treino.The recovery was run jointly by São Paulo's medical department, Dr. Moisés Cohen, who performed the surgery, and Lucas's personal physiotherapist, Taio Marques. According to the club, each phase only began once the previous one was complete, according to how the tendon responded to training loads.
